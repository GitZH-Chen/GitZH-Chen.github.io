[Fast and Stable Riemannian Metrics on SPD Manifolds via Cholesky Product Geometry](https://openreview.net/forum?id=5S8ruWKe8l) \\
**Ziheng Chen**, Yue Song, Xiao-Jun Wu, Nicu Sebe. 
[[Code](https://github.com/GitZH-Chen/PCM_BWCM)]
[[Slides](https://github.com/GitZH-Chen/PCM_BWCM/blob/main/ICLR26-PCM_BWCM-PPT.pdf)]
[[Poster](https://github.com/GitZH-Chen/PCM_BWCM/blob/main/ICLR26-PCM_BWCM-Poster.pdf)]
<strong><span class='show_paper_citations' data='4FA6C0AAAAAJ:qjMakFHDy7sC'></span></strong>
- Uncovers a product structure of Cholesky factors that enables convenient metric design.
- Introduces the Power--Cholesky Metric (PCM) and Bures--Wasserstein--Cholesky Metric (BWCM), with closed-form operators, computational efficiency, and improved numerical stability.
- Applies PCM and BWCM to Riemannian classifiers and residual blocks for SPD neural networks.
