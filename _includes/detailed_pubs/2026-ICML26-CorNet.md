[Riemannian Networks over Full-Rank Correlation Matrices](https://openreview.net/forum?id=8k4om4zj5E) \\
**Ziheng Chen**, Xiao-Jun Wu, Bernhard Schölkopf, Nicu Sebe.
[[Poster](https://icml.cc/media/PosterPDFs/ICML%202026/65936.png?t=1779479913.993183)]
- Extends MLR, fully connected, and convolutional layers to the correlation manifold under five geometries.
- Develops accurate backpropagation for Riemannian computations under OLM and LSM.
- Demonstrates the effectiveness of correlation embeddings and networks through comparisons with existing SPD and Grassmannian networks.
