I am an [ELLIS](https://ellis.eu) Ph.D. student affiliated with the [Multimedia and Human Understanding Group (MHUG)](https://mhug.disi.unitn.it/#/) at the [University of Trento, Italy](https://www.unitn.it/en), and currently visiting the [Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems (MPI-IS)](https://is.mpg.de/en) in Tübingen. I am advised by Prof. [Nicu Sebe](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=stFCYOAAAAAJ&hl=en) and Prof. [Bernhard Schölkopf](https://is.mpg.de/~bs). My research is supported by the [Google PhD Fellowship](https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/google-org/phd-fellows-2026/).

Before my Ph.D. studies, I received a B.A. degree in logistics management from Shandong University, Jinan, China, and an M.S. degree in computer science and technology from Jiangnan University, Wuxi, China, under the supervision of Prof. [Xiao-Jun Wu](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=5IST34sAAAAJ&hl=en) and Prof. [Josef Kittler](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/josef-kittler).

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and differential geometry, particularly deep learning on Riemannian manifolds. I also contribute to [Geoopt](https://github.com/geoopt/geoopt), a Riemannian optimization library for PyTorch. Check out [Awesome-Riemannian-Deep-Learning](https://github.com/GitZH-Chen/Awesome-Riemannian-Deep-Learning) for resources on deep learning over Riemannian spaces.
